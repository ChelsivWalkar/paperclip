# Biblical Post Design

## 1. Choose the moment

Pick one emotional "movement" for the post: comfort, joy, hope, gratitude, courage, belonging or love. One post = one truth = one feeling.

## 2. Build the brief

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Truth | One sentence of biblical truth |
| Verse | Exact text, reference, translation |
| Moment | The emotion and a human scene (a parent hugging a child, sunrise after rain, a family meal) |
| Headline | 3–7 words, large and readable on a phone |
| Caption | Under 220 characters, warm, ends with a gentle invitation |
| Alt text | Plain description for accessibility |

## 3. Visual rules

- Formats: 1080×1080 (feed) and 1080×1920 (status / story, keep text inside the central 1080×1420 safe zone).
- Max two typefaces: one expressive display face for the headline, one clean sans-serif for the verse.
- Contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for all text.
- Palettes: *Dawn* (warm gold, soft peach, cream) for hope; *Living Water* (teal, sky, white) for peace; *Neon Glory* (deep navy with neon cyan/magenta glow) for PESU and youth content.
- Light, faces and hands carry emotion; avoid stock clichés and AI images that look uncanny.
- Small Chelsiv.AI mark bottom-right; verse reference always visible.

## 4. Motion (optional)

For status videos: 6–10 seconds, slow fade-in of headline, gentle light sweep, verse appears last and holds 3 seconds.

## 5. Final checklist

- [ ] Verse passed `scripture-accuracy-check`
- [ ] One clear truth and one feeling
- [ ] Readable on a small phone screen
- [ ] No copyrighted images without rights
- [ ] Alt text written
- [ ] Sent to SIV as a draft for founder approval
