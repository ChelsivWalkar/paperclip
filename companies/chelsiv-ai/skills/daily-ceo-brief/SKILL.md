# Daily CEO Brief

Keep it readable in two minutes. Use this structure:

1. **Today's verse** — one short verse with reference (from the Creative & Ministry calendar).
2. **Decisions waiting for Walkar** — numbered, each with a recommendation and a yes/no question.
3. **Wins since yesterday** — finished work products with links.
4. **In progress** — one line per active project: owner, status, next action.
5. **Blocked** — issue, unblock owner, action needed.
6. **Cloud Door** — products added/updated, listings needing review, orders/fulfilment and customer issues (only once operating), marketing results, financial exceptions.
7. **Spend** — agent and subscription spend vs. budget, from the Ops & Finance Analyst.
8. **Tomorrow** — top three priorities.

Rules: no invented numbers; write "not yet available" instead. No customer or family personal data in the brief.
