# Family Privacy Guardrails

Core principle: **SIV should guide, not control.**

1. **Consent first** — every person (or a parent for a child) opts in explicitly. Consent is recorded and can be withdrawn anytime.
2. **Visible** — a person can always see what is shared and with whom. No hidden tracking.
3. **Pausable** — location or activity sharing has a one-tap pause.
4. **Minimum data** — collect only what the feature needs; keep health and food habits on-device or encrypted.
5. **Emergency contacts** — only used for the purpose the person chose.
6. **Children** — no profiling, no advertising, parental controls by default.
7. **No secrets in code** — credentials in a secure backend only, never in client code or public repos.
8. **Human help** — for safety, medical or legal emergencies, direct people to real emergency services and trusted humans.
